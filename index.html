Important calculation rules

Base wins: the opponent's published or supplemental wins at the last successful refresh. District 1 schools are prefilled from official standings. For schools outside District 1, this field may be blank until confirmed.

Additional: predicted wins from games not already selected in What-if games. Do not count a game twice.

Class: official PIAA assigned football classes are preloaded for PA schools in Districts 3, 11 and 12. For non-PIAA schools, enter the verified MALE enrollment in grades 10–12; the class will be calculated automatically from the 2026 thresholds.

Enrollment totals for all grades, or male totals for 9–12, do not prove the required male 10–12 count. Signed forms must be reviewed by District One; manual classifications are scenario assumptions until verified.

Unknown wins or classifications can make projected points incomplete. The official base totals are retained exactly.
